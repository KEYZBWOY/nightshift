Privacy

Night Shift is a free browser game. We don't ask you to make an account, and we don't collect your name, email address or other personal details.

What stays on your device: your progress (level, unlocks, story stars, settings) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Online play: when online matches are added, the game connects you to other players' browsers to play together. It shares only what the match needs, such as your operator name and your moves.

Advertising: we may show ads in the future. Ad partners may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads, under their own privacy policies. This page will be updated when that happens.

Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions: contact us through the page where you found the game.
